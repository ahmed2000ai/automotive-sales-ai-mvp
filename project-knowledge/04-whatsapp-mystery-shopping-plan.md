# WhatsApp Mystery-Shopping Plan

**File:** `04-whatsapp-mystery-shopping-plan.md`  
**Purpose:** Define the standardized WhatsApp mystery-shopping experiment used to test how Saudi premium automotive protection businesses handle a realistic high-value PPF enquiry.

---

## 1. Experiment Objective

The purpose of this experiment was to move beyond public website research and observe the **actual WhatsApp sales process** used by premium automotive protection businesses.

The main questions were:

- How quickly do businesses respond?
- Is the first response automated or human?
- Does the response understand the customer's actual message?
- Does the salesperson qualify the lead?
- Are all customer questions answered?
- Is the customer moved toward booking?
- Is the quote followed up?
- Is the follow-up personalized?
- Does the business appear to manage the enquiry as a sales opportunity or merely as a chat?

The experiment was designed to test the emerging business hypothesis:

> **A significant sales-conversion opportunity exists after the WhatsApp enquiry arrives.**

---

## 2. Test Vehicle

Use the user's actual vehicle:

> **Nissan Patrol 2025**

This was considered ideal because:

- it is a real vehicle owned by the user
- it is a high-value vehicle
- full-body PPF is a credible purchase
- the enquiry should be treated as commercially serious
- using the same vehicle across businesses creates a fair comparison

---

## 3. Standardized Initial Enquiry

Send the same core message to every selected business.

### Standard Arabic Message

> السلام عليكم،  
> عندي نيسان باترول 2025 وأفكر أركب حماية PPF كاملة للسيارة.  
> ممكن أعرف كم السعر عندكم حالياً، ونوع الفيلم المستخدم ومدة الضمان؟ وكم يحتاج التركيب تقريباً؟

This asks for four explicit things:

1. current price
2. film type / brand
3. warranty duration
4. approximate installation time

The wording is intentionally:

- realistic
- concise
- commercially serious
- not overly technical
- not aggressive
- not price-only

---

## 4. Why the Same Message Must Be Used

Using the same enquiry across all businesses allows comparison of:

- response time
- completeness
- qualification
- salesmanship
- automation quality
- follow-up discipline

Do not significantly customize the first message for each company.

Otherwise the experiment becomes less comparable.

---

## 5. Businesses Selected for the First Wave

The initial recommendation was to contact approximately eight Jeddah businesses rather than all 30 researched companies.

The first wave included:

- Liquidglass Protect
- Viper Smart Films
- Pro Master
- VIP Car Care Jeddah
- KMQ - Madinah Road Branch
- Superior Choice
- LCE Automotive Protection Boutique
- DRIVEN

Reason for limiting the sample:

> Eight businesses were sufficient to expose different sales-process patterns while keeping the experiment manageable.

---

## 6. Why Jeddah Was Used First

Jeddah was chosen as the first validation market because:

- the user is locally based in the area
- there are many premium automotive protection businesses
- later face-to-face pilot discussions would be easier
- the market contains both digitally mature and less structured operators

---

## 7. Whether to Use a Personal Mobile Number

Recommended:

> **Yes, use a real Saudi mobile number and the user's real first name.**

Preferred setup:

- real number
- real first name
- normal personal WhatsApp profile

Avoid:

- fake identity
- fake vehicle ownership
- unnecessary deception

If the WhatsApp profile prominently displays:

- company title
- CEO / executive position
- business logo
- company website

then a separate personal number would be preferable to avoid the business treating the enquiry differently.

---

## 8. Important Ethical Boundary

Do not:

- make a fake booking
- reserve a workshop slot you do not intend to use
- no-show
- create unnecessary work for the business
- falsely claim payment or purchase intent beyond normal consideration

The purpose is to test the **sales process**, not disrupt operations.

---

## 9. Natural Reply Strategy

The customer should respond naturally enough to move the conversation forward, but should not artificially keep the salesperson engaged.

If the salesperson gives the price / details and the conversation reaches a normal consideration stage, use:

> تمام يعطيك العافية، أفكر وأرجع لك.

Alternative used in some conversations:

> تمام افكر و ارد لك خبر

The key idea:

> Put the salesperson into a realistic “customer is considering” state and then stop.

This creates the opportunity to observe natural follow-up.

---

## 10. If the Business Asks a Relevant Qualification Question

Answer truthfully and briefly.

Example:

If asked:

> تبي الحماية لامع أو مطفي؟

Answer:

> مطفي

Do not over-explain.

The goal is to see what the salesperson does next.

---

## 11. If the Business Asks Redundant Questions

If automation asks for information already supplied, such as:

- vehicle model
- service required

record the redundancy.

If needed to continue the test, provide the information once.

This behavior itself is important evidence.

Example:

Original message already contains:

- Nissan Patrol 2025
- full PPF

If the bot asks:

> What vehicle?  
> What service?

record:

> **Context understanding failure**

---

## 12. Do Not Chase Non-Responders Immediately

If a business does not respond:

- do not immediately send another message
- record the timestamp
- wait

This allows measurement of:

- delayed response
- missed enquiry
- later follow-up
- manual vs automated handling

## 14. Additional Metrics Added During the Experiment

As the experiment progressed, additional useful metrics emerged.

### Automated Acknowledgement Time

How quickly did any automated response arrive?

### Meaningful Response Time

How long until the customer received a response that actually addressed the enquiry?

This distinction became especially important after Liquidglass.

Example concept:

> Auto acknowledgement: 3 seconds  
> Meaningful response: 5h 26m

---

## 15. Question Completion Measurement

Because the standard enquiry contains four explicit questions, record whether each was answered.

### Customer Asked

- price
- film type
- warranty
- installation duration

Track:

- Price: answered / unanswered
- Film: answered / unanswered
- Warranty: answered / unanswered
- Installation duration: answered / unanswered

This metric later became one of the most important product requirements.

---

## 16. Follow-Up Test

The original plan was to observe at roughly:

- 24 hours
- 3 days
- 7 days

without prompting the business.

The purpose:

> Determine whether a high-value PPF lead remains visible in the salesperson's process after the customer goes quiet.

Important:

The experiment later showed that waiting the full seven days was **not necessary before progressing to MVP/customer validation**.

The remaining follow-up data became useful supporting evidence rather than a blocker.

---

## 17. What Counts as Strong Follow-Up

Weak example:

> Still interested?

Better example:

> أستاذ أحمد، حبيت أتابع معك بخصوص حماية الـPPF للباترول 2025، إذا حاب أشرح لك الفرق بين الخيارات أو نرتب لك موعد أنا حاضر.

Strong follow-up should ideally:

- reference the prior conversation
- reference the service / package
- invite an objection
- offer help
- move toward a next step
- avoid excessive pressure

---

## 18. What to Save

Do not save only copied text.

Also retain:

- screenshots
- images
- brochures
- price sheets
- voice-note transcriptions
- package cards
- timestamps

Reason:

Important information may be contained in:

- images
- PDFs
- voice messages
- offer cards

A text-only transcript may incorrectly make it appear that information was missing.

## 19. Analysis Framework

Each business should ultimately be classified across these dimensions.

### A. Context Understanding

Did the system / salesperson understand what the customer already said?

### B. Completeness

Were the original questions answered?

### C. Qualification

Did the salesperson ask useful questions that improve recommendation quality?

### D. Salesmanship

Did they:

- build trust
- differentiate
- provide evidence
- address objections
- reduce friction

### E. Conversion Movement

Did they attempt to move the lead toward:

- appointment
- visit
- booking
- deposit
- purchase decision

### F. Follow-Up Discipline

Did the business follow up after the customer went silent?

### G. Management Visibility Potential

Would an owner be able to understand:

- response time
- lead stage
- salesperson activity
- conversion outcome

---

## 20. Initial Product Hypotheses Being Tested

The experiment was designed to test several possible problems.

### Hypothesis 1

Businesses lose high-value leads because they do not follow up.

### Hypothesis 2

Automation exists but often does not understand the customer's message.

### Hypothesis 3

Salespeople frequently leave customer questions unanswered.

### Hypothesis 4

Response quality varies heavily between employees.

### Hypothesis 5

Management lacks visibility into:

- lead handling
- response time
- follow-up
- conversion

### Hypothesis 6

A vertical product could improve sales without replacing the salesperson.

---

## 21. Expected Product Direction if Hypotheses Were Confirmed

If the experiment validated these problems, the proposed product would focus on:

- WhatsApp context understanding
- lead extraction
- opportunity creation
- question-completion checking
- meaningful-response SLA
- structured follow-up
- salesperson assistance
- manager dashboards
- conversion reporting
- lead-source attribution

---

## 22. What the Experiment Was Not Designed to Prove

The mystery-shopping test alone cannot prove:

- willingness to pay
- exact market size
- exact subscription price
- implementation complexity
- customer retention
- profitability
- whether businesses will replace existing tools

These require:

> **direct owner / manager interviews and real pilot offers**

That became the next phase once enough sales-process evidence was collected.
